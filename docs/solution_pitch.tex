\section{The Problem: Paper Registers \& Delayed Escalation}
India's 1M+ Accredited Social Health Activists (\textbf{ASHAs}) conduct doorstep maternal and general health visits across rural villages, often operating on basic 2G feature phones in high-noise environments. Vital readings (blood pressure, hemoglobin, gestational age, danger signs) are written into paper registers and batch-entered days later at the Primary Health Centre (\textbf{PHC}). By the time severe preeclampsia ($\text{BP} \ge 160/110\text{ mmHg}$) or critical anemia ($\text{Hb} < 5.0\text{ g/dL}$) is reviewed by a Medical Officer, the window for early intervention has often closed.

\section{The Solution: ASHA-Vani}
\textbf{ASHA-Vani} turns any basic feature phone or PSTN line into a real-time, voice-verified clinical register and early-warning system. An ASHA worker dials in (via an Exotel PSTN bridge or the built-in 8\,kHz Nokia simulator) and speaks naturally in \textbf{Hindi or Hinglish}. Within milliseconds per turn, ASHA-Vani:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Streams 8\,kHz telephone audio through \textbf{Gnani Prisma STT} to transcribe code-mixed Hindi/Hinglish and Devanagari numerals.
  \item Extracts structured clinical fields into a stateful \textbf{7-step LangGraph intake} (\texttt{CallState}) using Groq Llama 3.3 70B (with 8B/deterministic regex fallback).
  \item Enforces \textbf{hard physiological bounds} and runs a \textbf{deterministic clinical rule engine (\texttt{R1--R8})}.
  \item Speaks a \textbf{digit-by-digit Hindi/Hinglish readback} via \textbf{Gnani Timbre v2.5 TTS} and commits the record as \texttt{VERIFIED} only after the ASHA confirms.
  \item Streams turn-by-turn updates to the \textbf{PHC Clinical Register Console} for 1-click Medical Officer home dispatch (\texttt{108} Ambulance, ANM Mobile, or Home Follow-up).
\end{itemize}

\section{Core Gnani.ai Voice Integration}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{8\,kHz Telephony Conditioning (\texttt{voice/audio.py}):} Incoming audio is resampled to 8\,kHz mono PCM16, band-pass filtered to PSTN telephone band ($300\text{--}3400\text{ Hz}$), $\mu$-law companded, and sliced into exact \textbf{1024-byte (64\,ms)} frames. Includes calibrated SNR field-noise injection ($15\text{ dB}$ and $5\text{ dB}$) to validate rural acoustic resilience.
  \item \textbf{Gnani Prisma Streaming STT (\texttt{voice/stt.py}):} Bi-directional WebSocket streaming with dual End-of-Utterance (\textbf{EOU}) detection—triggering on either a $\sim 1.2\text{ s}$ silence window or the trailing radio keyword \texttt{``over''} / \texttt{``ओवर''} (automatically stripped prior to extraction). Supports DTMF keypad fallback (\texttt{0--9, *, \#}) when voice conditions are extreme.
  \item \textbf{Gnani Timbre v2.5 TTS (\texttt{voice/tts.py}):} Synthesizes natural Hindi/Hinglish prompts (\texttt{Poorvi} / \texttt{Nalini} voices) at 8\,kHz PCM/WAV. Uses an in-memory fixed-script cache so recurring prompts and repeat requests incur zero redundant synthesis latency or API cost.
  \item \textbf{Half-Duplex Anti-Echo Gate (\texttt{voice/session.py}):} Drops all incoming microphone frames while Timbre TTS is playing plus a strict \textbf{200\,ms acoustic tail guard} after playback finishes, preventing speaker-to-mic self-triggering on loudspeaker phones.
\end{itemize}

\section{Clinical Safety Architecture}
ASHA-Vani is strictly a \textbf{decision-support and early-warning pipeline}, never an autonomous diagnosis engine:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=12pt,itemsep=1pt,topsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{The LLM Extracts; Deterministic Code Decides:} The language model only populates candidate slots (\texttt{ExtractedVisit}); risk classification is 100\% rule-based (\texttt{triage.py}).
  \item \textbf{Refuse to Guess (Hard/Soft Range Guards):} Impossible ASR/speech artifacts (e.g., $\text{BP } 420/110\text{ mmHg}$, $\text{Hb } 28\text{ g/dL}$, or $\text{systolic} \le \text{diastolic}$) are rejected as \texttt{ERROR}, cleared to \texttt{None}, and routed to a targeted spoken repeat prompt—without masking co-occurring critical danger signs.
  \item \textbf{Alert Early, Commit Late:} High-risk intakes appear on the PHC console immediately on Turn 1 marked \texttt{UNVERIFIED (In Call)} so officers have early situational awareness, flipping to \texttt{VERIFIED} only after the ASHA confirms the spoken readback (\emph{``Haan, sahi hai''}).
\end{enumerate}
