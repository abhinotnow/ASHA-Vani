ASHA-Vani
A multilingual phone call that turns a village home visit into a confirmed record for the PHC.
GNANI GREAT INDIAN AI CHALLENGE

When an ASHA hears a warning sign in a village home, the PHC should hear it too—while the visit is still fresh.

Nearly one million ASHAs are the first point of contact for rural families across India, yet visit notes still start on paper and mobile apps require typing and smartphone data. ASHA-Vani lets any worker report from a basic feature phone simply by speaking naturally in Hindi, Tamil, or Telugu (including code-mixed Hinglish, Tanglish, and Telglish), built on a drop-in locale architecture ready to scale across all 10 Gnani Indian languages.

The Gap From village visit to PHC awareness

A high blood-pressure reading or danger symptom matters in the moment, but on paper it can wait days to reach the Primary Health Centre. With ASHA-Vani, callers dial a toll-free line, receive a warm trilingual greeting ("Namaste! Vanakkam! Namaskaram!"), verify their 3-digit ASHA ID by voice or keypad, and report the visit live in their own language.

How the Call Becomes a Record

4-Step Multilingual Loop
01
Listen Gnani Prisma STT
8 kHz speech in Hindi, Tamil, or Telugu (and Hinglish/Tanglish/Telglish) streams through an Energy VAD gate so the worker is never cut off mid-sentence.
02
Understand Gnani Evon v3.3 LLM
Phone and Aadhaar numbers are redacted pre-LLM; Evon extracts patient, vitals ("dedh sau by pachhanave" → 150/95), and symptoms with enable_thinking=False, backed by Groq/regex failover.
03
Check & read back Gnani Timbre v2.5 TTS
Bounds are checked before speaking back captured numbers digit-by-digit in the worker's native voice (Poorvi Hindi, Asmita Tamil, Suhana Telugu) with half-duplex anti-echo muting.
04
Confirm & share PHC Register Console
The ASHA confirms or corrects any detail aloud. Only after spoken confirmation does the PHC dashboard mark the visit verified alongside the full-call WAV audio.

Multilingual by design & built for safety

Pan-India languages: Auto-detects Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu via indic-auto (or mid-call via Keypad 9), and scales to 10 Indian languages using JSON locales while transliterating names to English Title Case for doctors.

Human-in-the-loop safety: Unclear measurements trigger a polite repeat, records stay unverified until confirmed aloud, and PHC staff always own clinical follow-up and referral decisions.

Future Prospects & Scalability
  • Household Medicine Kit Tracking: Log IFA, ORS, Zinc, Calcium, and TT supplies per household caretaker to prevent village stockouts.
  • Automated Due-Lists & HBNC: Auto-generate monthly ANC and child vaccination due-lists alongside 6-visit newborn follow-ups.
  • Cluster PHC + SMS Receipts: One PHC operator serves 10–25 villages while confirmed calls send local-script SMS receipts for ASHA incentives.
