ASHA-Vani: Voice-First Clinical Triage & Register

Real-time Hindi/Hinglish decision support & early-warning ledger for rural Indian ASHA workers · Powered by Gnani Prisma STT & Gnani Timbre v2.5 TTS
1-PAGE SOLUTION PITCH
1. The Rural Clinical Bottleneck PROBLEM

Over 1 million rural ASHA workers conduct doorstep maternal and general health visits across India, often using basic 2G feature phones in noisy village environments. Vital signs (blood pressure, hemoglobin, gestational weeks, danger signs) are written into paper registers and digitized days later at the Primary Health Centre (PHC). By the time severe preeclampsia (BP ≥ 160/110) or critical anemia (Hb < 5.0 g/dL) is spotted by a Medical Officer, the window for early referral has often closed.

2. The ASHA-Vani Solution OVERVIEW

ASHA-Vani turns any basic feature phone or Exotel PSTN line into a voice-verified clinical register. The ASHA dials in and speaks naturally in Hindi or Hinglish (or dials DTMF keys when network audio is severe). Each spoken turn streams through Gnani Prisma STT, updates a 7-step LangGraph intake state machine, runs hard physiological range checks and deterministic clinical rules (R1–R8), speaks a digit-by-digit confirm-back via Gnani Timbre TTS, and streams live updates to the PHC Medical Officer Console.

3. End-to-End Voice Pipeline ARCHITECTURE
[2G Handset / Exotel PSTN] → 8 kHz PCM16 · 300-3400 Hz · μ-law (1024B frames)
↓ Half-Duplex Gate (mutes mic during TTS + 200 ms acoustic tail)
[Gnani Prisma STT] → EOU Detector (~1.2s pause OR trailing "over")
↓ TurnRunner Queue (strictly ordered per session_id)
[LangGraph CallState] → Extract (Llama 3.3 70B) → Validate → Triage (R1-R8)
↓ Digit-by-digit readback (→ [Gnani Timbre v2.5 TTS] w/ WAV cache)
[PHC Register Console + Supabase] → 1-Click 108 / ANM Home Dispatch
4. Deep Gnani.ai Integration PRISMA + TIMBRE
  • 8 kHz Telephony Conditioning (voice/audio.py): Resamples browser/PSTN audio to 8 kHz mono PCM16, applies 300–3400 Hz bandpass filtering, μ-law companding, exact 1024-byte (64 ms) framing, and calibrated SNR noise injection (15 dB / 5 dB).
  • Gnani Prisma STT (voice/stt.py): Streaming Hindi/Hinglish & Devanagari recognition with dual End-of-Utterance detection (1.2s silence or trailing "over" keyword stripped before extraction).
  • Gnani Timbre v2.5 TTS (voice/tts.py): Low-latency 8 kHz PCM/WAV synthesis (Poorvi/Nalini voices) with an in-memory fixed-script cache so greeting and follow-up prompts cost zero extra synthesis latency.
  • Half-Duplex Anti-Echo Gate (voice/session.py): Drops incoming mic frames during TTS playback plus a 200 ms tail mute so loudspeaker handsets never re-transcribe their own prompts.
5. Clinical Safety Invariants SAFETY BY DESIGN
  • The LLM Extracts; Deterministic Code Decides: The model only extracts candidate slots (ExtractedVisit). Risk level is never assigned by an LLM—it is computed 100% deterministically in triage.py.
  • Refuse to Guess (Hard/Soft Range Guards): Impossible ASR values (e.g., BP 420/110, Hb 28.0, or systolic ≤ diastolic) fail hard validation (ERROR), are cleared to None, and trigger a targeted repeat question without hiding co-occurring critical symptoms.
  • Alert Early, Commit Late: High-risk cases appear on the PHC console on Turn 1 marked UNVERIFIED (In Call) for early awareness, flipping to VERIFIED only after the ASHA confirms the spoken readback ("Haan, sahi hai").
  • General & Maternal Pathways: If a patient is non-pregnant (is_pregnant=False), the state machine automatically skips gestational weeks and evaluates general vitals, severe anemia, fever, and neurological danger signs.

R2 CRITICAL Elevated BP (≥ 140/90) + severe headache, blurred vision, epigastric pain, or facial edema
